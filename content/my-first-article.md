Generating sustainable passive income is the ultimate milestone of financial freedom. For younger investors, starting early is your ultimate superpower.

## Visualizing the Growth: The Passive Income Snowball

When you invest in dividend-paying stocks, you can automatically activate a **Dividend Reinvestment Plan (DRIP)**. Instead of spending the cash payouts, your brokerage automatically uses them to purchase fractional shares of the same company.

![Chart Example](/images/cover.png)

## Why Dividend Investing Works

Companies returning capital to shareholders outperform over the long term with far less volatility.

| Feature | High-Yield Savings (HYSA) | Dividend Growth Stocks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Principal Growth** | None. Your $1,000 will always be just $1,000. | High Potential. The stock price can appreciate. |
| **Payout Adjustments** | Variable. Banks lower APY when rates drop. | Compounding. Companies intentionally raise payouts annually. |

## Execution Plan

1. Open a brokerage account.
2. Establish your strategy split.
3. Turn on automatic DRIP settings.
